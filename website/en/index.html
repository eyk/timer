1–999 minutesUp to ten timersNo accounts. No ads.

Open with the keyboard shortcut shown in the app. Type minutes and press Enter. The window disappears; your timer keeps running. When time is up, an audible and visible alarm brings you back. Press Enter to acknowledge.

Timers last for the current session. During sleep, an overdue alarm waits until your computer wakes. No timer data is sent to us.
