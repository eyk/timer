Privacy

Publisher: Walter Seethaler (eyk).

Eyk Timer runs locally and sends no timer, personal or usage data to the developer. No accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Timers are kept in memory and discarded when the app quits.

This website uses no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. GitHub Pages processes technical connection data, including IP addresses, under its privacy statement. GitHub downloads and support are separate services. Windows and WebView2 are subject to their providers’ terms.

Contact: GitHub Issues. Do not post personal or confidential information publicly.
